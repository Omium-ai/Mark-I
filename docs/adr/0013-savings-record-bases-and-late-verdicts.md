# ADR-0013 — The savings record is authoritative: the knee's bases ride the bundle and the decision, and late verdicts restate, never mutate

- **Status:** Proposed — written 2026-09-26 by the L2 follow-up lane at the lead's direction (`docs/design/16-release-savings-rm.md` §L2, the two shortcuts L2 left: "knee_basis inferred at edge until ADR-0013" and "savings.v2 misses post-window verdicts"); awaiting the founder's signature at the release readiness review
- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Deciders:** Anurag Upadhyay (founder), Omium
- **Reversibility:** cheap for the arithmetic, one-way for the wire. `route.knee.basis` and `GuardDecision.cost_basis` are additive (an optional bundle key, proto field 10) and every reader treats their absence as `unknown`; the savings fold and templates are versioned (`savings.v3`, `team_device_totals.v2`, `app_sessions.v10`) and hold no state. An edge older than this release refuses a bundle carrying the key (strict maps, 07 rule 3), so the key is published only to fleets that decode it (§8); the columns it adds (m1_104, m1_ch_003) are additive.
- **Blocks:** quoting a savings figure split by cost basis; any bundle carrying `anchor_grounded` (the anchor-grounded knee fit of the release research, Phase 3, needs no further wire change once this lands); a weekly digest whose figure a later verdict can contradict.

## Context

Doc 16 ruling 4 makes "tokens saved against plain Claude Code" the release's headline number. Lane L2 built it (`savings.v2`) and left two shortcuts, each marked in code:

1. **The knee's provenance was guessed at the edge.** ADR-0012 §5 records `GuardDecision.knee_basis` (`prior` | `anchor_grounded` | `certified`), but no bundle said where its rows' p came from, so `verdict/frombundle.go` inferred it: fewer than three correlation groups ⇒ `prior`, else `certified`. The inference was exact only while exactly two producers existed (the bootstrap prior and the compiler's certified fit) and it cannot name `anchor_grounded` at all.
2. **The decision's cost basis was not on the record.** The knee compares against c(r) under the bundle's `route.knee.cost_basis` (06 §8.4), but the `GuardDecision` carried no basis. The ledger's `cost_basis` column is stamped at ingest from the tenant's *current* regime (chproj `Stamped`), the bootstrap bundle defaults a regime-less tenant to `quota`, and the report's `cost_basis=auto` defaults the same tenant to `usd`: three answers for the basis of one number, none of them the one the decision was priced under.
3. **Late verdicts were outside the window.** `savings.v2` read verdicts and post-block usage only inside `[from, to]`. Verdicts routinely land later: a slow anchor's `REVISED`, the adjudicator's `REVISED`/`CERTIFIED` dated at its readjudication window close, a nightly judge or RM feeding that run. A past window (the weekly digest's Mon..Sun, anything on `POST /query`) resolved its blocks without them. On a rolling window the figure did move, but silently: a day the report already graded `closed` (ADR-0010's closed/live split, I2) changed with no mark.

## Decision

1. **The bundle says where p came from.** `route.knee` gains an optional closed enum `basis`: `prior` | `anchor_grounded` | `certified`. The producer that fitted the rows stamps it: controld's bootstrap bundle `prior` (Paper I's prior, `policydist/bootstrap.go`); intel's policy compiler `certified` (Wilson LCBs over 06 §10.3's certified set, `knee.py`); `anchor_grounded` is reserved for the anchor-grounded fit. Both edge decoders (`mark1-policy`, `go/mark1d/internal/policy`), the controld publisher (`ValidateSections`) and intel's wire model accept it; any other word is `M1E-3001` (schema, never the paged `M1E-3005`). Frozen vectors `knee_basis` (accept) and `knee_basis_bad` (reject) lock both decoders (37 vectors).
2. **The edge never infers.** A bundle without the key is recorded as `unknown`: `KneeState.Basis`, `GuardDecision.knee_basis = "unknown"`, and the gate's reason words it "p(r), basis unrecorded" — neither "prior p(r)" nor p̂_LCB, because it is neither claim. The local arrest SSE row keeps the three-word enum older apps parse and carries `knee_basis: null` beside a `round` (an evaluable knee arrest; a block-cap arrest has neither), which the app words "p(r)". The route-bundle read (`appapi`, `route_bundle.json`) answers `knee.basis`, `unknown` when absent, and the Route screen heads the p column by it.
3. **The decision carries the basis it was priced under.** `GuardDecision.cost_basis` (proto field 10) is the applied bundle's `route.knee.cost_basis`, stamped on every verify-plane decision (block, knee arrest, block cap) from the same applied matrices as `bundle_seq`. Empty means an edge that predates the field (or no bundle, where the gate decides nothing). Ingest refuses a `knee_basis` outside {`""`, prior, anchor_grounded, certified, unknown} or a `cost_basis` outside {`""`, usd, quota} as `M1E-2006` `guard_basis`, naming the event. The ledger's `cost_basis` column keeps its meaning (the tenant's regime at ingest); the decision's own basis is read from its payload.
4. **Savings count by the decision's basis, honestly labelled.** `savings.v3` gives each task the basis of its first decision (`decision_basis`, `unknown` when empty). `tokens_saved.total` stays the whole net (tokens are tokens, and Sessions, Team and Home keep agreeing with Reports); `tokens_saved.by_cost_basis` splits it per basis and every surface that shows the split names `unknown` ("recorded before Mark 1 stamped a cost basis") and any basis other than the report's. `tokens_saved.certified` counts only certified tasks **decided under the report's own basis**: an `unknown` or other-basis decision is never certified.
5. **Membership is the decision; resolution is not windowed.** A task belongs to a window when its verify-plane decision is dated inside `[from, to]`. Its terminal verdict and its post-block usage are read through today, so a window resolves with every verdict the ledger holds when it is read. `team_device_totals.v2` does the same for `tokens_saved` only; its sessions, tokens and verified tasks stay inside `[from, to]`.
6. **A closed day is restated, never mutated.** For a task whose decision day has closed (UTC day end + 2 h, the rollup grace), the report also computes the net as the ledger stood at that close (the rows controld had received by then, §11). Where the two differ, the task counts in `tokens_saved.restated {tasks, total}`: the part of the net that moved after the day closed, shown beside the net ("Of which restated by verdicts that arrived after the day closed"). An open day is live and restates nothing. A sent weekly digest cannot be restated in place, so the send ledger keeps the figure it mailed (`m1_notification_sends.tokens_saved`, m1_102) and the next week's email recomputes that week and carries "Last week's tokens saved, restated: X (was Y)" when it moved.
7. **Every figure is idempotent.** Each number is a pure function of the deduplicated ledger (`LIMIT 1 BY event_id`; the adjudicator's event ids are deterministic) and of the clock only as far as deciding which days have closed. A rerun, a replayed batch or a re-posted adjudication answers the same figures; the digest keeps its at-most-once send per person-week.
8. **No edge is ever handed a key it cannot decode (lead ruling, rollout is cloud-first).** The edge sends `X-Mark1-Bundle-Features: knee_basis` on every bundle request; controld keeps each device's latest word (`m1_devices.bundle_features`; NULL = has not polled since it enrolled, `{}` = polled and advertised nothing; m1_104 marks every device enrolled before it `{}`). A publication carries `route.knee.basis` only when no non-revoked device of the tenant is known not to decode it; otherwise the key is withheld from the signed bytes and the producer's word is kept (`policy_bundles.knee_basis_intended`, beside `knee_basis`, what the bytes carry). A poll that did not advertise the word is never served a head carrying the key: the tenant's heads are republished without it first, in the same request. A poll that changes a device's word, and every rollout sweep, run the same idempotent resync, so a fleet that now decodes the key gets it at the next publication. Promotion and rollback carry the producer's word forward. A mixed fleet reads "basis unrecorded" exactly as before this release.
9. **The pre-ADR-0013 bootstrap is re-issued, never rewritten.** A tenant whose `stable` head is still seq 1 with no recorded producer word gets the current bootstrap (route.knee.basis `prior`, tests their own clique, the resolved regime) published once at seq 2 by the resync step: a normal publication (seq + 1, prev_hash chained, transparency-log leaf, receipt), under the §8 gate (withheld from a mixed fleet, added when it upgrades). A second run publishes nothing.
10. **One regime rule.** `querychoke.Resolve` — the tenant's m1_075 row, else a subscription (`quota`) at unit value — is what the bootstrap bundle prices its knee under and what the report's `cost_basis=auto` reads; there is no second default.
11. **"As it stood at the close" is what controld had received.** `m1p_events.received_at` (m1_ch_003) is the projector's receipt time: the request's on live ingest, the landing object's S3 `LastModified` on replay (the replay log is written once, at receipt), so a replay reproduces it to the second. The `*_at_close` reads count only rows received before the decision day's close — a block that arrived after it was not in the figure at all — so an edge offline over midnight produces a visible restatement, never a silent move.

## Consequences

- **Cloud first is safe.** The gate (§8) is what makes it so: before any edge of a tenant advertises the word, every publication withholds the key, and an old edge that joins a fleet that already has it is served a republication without it. Each fleet change can cost one publication; a device that has not polled since enrolling is not counted against the key, so a new tenant's first bootstrap carries it and only an old edge's first poll republishes.
- **Resync publications are real publications.** A republished `canary` head is a new head, so a rollout in flight restarts on it. The per-tenant lock is per process: two pods resyncing at once can publish the same content twice, which is harmless (identical bytes under a higher seq).
- **History is `unknown` until the edge updates.** Every decision recorded before this release has no `cost_basis`; the split names it, the total still counts it, the certified figure never does.
- **Restatement is by receipt, to the second.** `ingested_at` could not serve (it is the `ReplacingMergeTree` version column, re-stamped by every replay insert); `received_at` is, and on replay it is S3's second-precision `LastModified`, so a row received within a second of a close can land on either side of it. Rows projected before m1_ch_003 read `ingested_at` through the column default: their latest ingest, the best evidence they have.
- **The digest restates one week back, to someone who worked this week.** A figure that moves again later, or for a person idle the following week, reaches no email; the app's report always shows the live figure with its restated part.
- **Scans run to today.** A past window's savings read scans from `from` to the present. Report windows already end today; the digest's extra read is one more week per tenant per Monday.
- Rejected: keeping the edge inference (it cannot name `anchor_grounded` and silently guesses about a bundle it did not compile); defaulting an absent key to `prior` (a compiled pre-key bundle is a fit, and calling it a prior is a false statement in the other direction); excluding `unknown` decisions from the total (every decision before this release is `unknown`, so the headline would drop to zero on upgrade and Sessions would disagree with Reports); re-deriving the basis from the tenant's current regime (a regime change would re-label decisions priced under the old one); writing savings into a closed rollup and editing it (ADR-0010 forbids editing a written row, and a live read from the ledger needs no second store).
